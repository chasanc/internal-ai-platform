# hr-jd-drafts runbook

Drafts a job description from a hiring manager's bullet points. A recruiter always edits and publishes; nothing is posted automatically.

- **Stop it:** revoke its gateway key (`hr-jd-drafts`).
- **Quality:** track how much recruiters rewrite drafts; heavy rewrites mean the prompt needs work.
- **Watch for:** biased or exclusionary wording; the reviewer is the control, so keep the review step.
- **Owner on call:** the mailbox in the registry entry.
