# marketing-campaign-brief runbook

Turns a campaign idea and public product facts into a one-page brief. A marketer edits it; nothing is published automatically.

- **Stop it:** revoke its gateway key (`marketing-campaign-brief`).
- **Quality:** a marketer spot-checks claims against the source facts; the tool must not invent product claims.
- **Data:** public information only. Do not paste customer or unreleased data into it.
- **Owner on call:** the mailbox in the registry entry.
